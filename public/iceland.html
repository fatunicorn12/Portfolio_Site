Day 3
Hot River + South Coast
1AMReykjadalur hike
From Hveragerði, ~3 hrs round trip. Ends at a geothermal river you can swim in. Bring swimsuit, towel, change of clothes.
2Stop in Selfoss
Famous hot dog stand.
3PMSkógafoss waterfall
Walk to base or climb the stairs alongside for a top view.
4Reynisfjara black sand beach
⚠ Do not approach the water. Sneaker waves are unpredictable and have killed tourists. Stay well back. Basalt columns, sea stacks.
5sleepVík
Far from light pollution — excellent northern lights conditions.
